## Researching a Software Failure
In december 2020, a game called cyberpunk 2077 released by a studio called CD Projekt Red who had previous experience on the witcher series of games, the game released with a serious number of software failures, especially on the previous generation of consoles. players reported constant crashes, frame rates dropping graphical glitches, questlines completly unplayable and many other bugs in the game that made the game difficult to play. Sony even took the game off of the playstation store temporarily and refunded customers. The company acknowledged the state of the game and realised they had not tested it well enough before release. The games complexity and it releasing on many platforms without proper testing on each platform contributed to the difficulties. This incident demonstartes how poor optimisation and testing can result in major software failures.

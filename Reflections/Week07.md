## Short Memo
To:Manager \
Subject:Appropriate use of software productivity data \
\
Software productivity data such as lines of code (LOC), function points and development time can be used at a team level to estimate future projects, identify trends and improve time to complete.\
However this data should not be sued to rank individual developers or punish them for lower productivity. Individual performance can be affected by task complexity, experience code quality and many more factors that raw metrics cannot capture.\
Even though the can be used at a team level the likes of POC cannot be fully relied on as even in teams that have high POC may not be doing everything as effectively as possible. Using the example of the assemble line vs high level earlier the assembly had a much higher POC but took much more time to complete the project rather than the high level, this would have cost the company more as a result.

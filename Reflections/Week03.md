*Task 1 Handout A-CampusEats Under Agile*

1. Requirements: New feature is added to backlog with clear acceptance criteria rather than requiring all original requirements to be reopened.
2. Design: The team reviews all existing designs and updates any part that is affected by the new change
3. Implementation: Developers break the work into small tasks and implements them accordingly.
4. UI: previously completed screens need to change to include the new ordering process.
5. Testing: The team test the new features during development and make sure all features work together.
6. Client Feedback: At the end of a sprint, the team demonstrates the changes and gains feedback before continuing
7. Schedule: The team focuses on the backlog and may move to less important features later to delay things as little as possible.
